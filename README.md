# 🏦 Bank Customer Churn Analytics Dashboard | Power BI Project

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze **customer churn, retention, and risk monitoring** for a retail banking environment.  
This project transforms raw banking data into actionable insights that help identify high-risk customers, understand churn drivers, and support data-driven retention strategies.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Customer churn is one of the biggest challenges in the banking industry. Losing customers directly impacts revenue, profitability, and long-term growth.

This dashboard provides a **data-driven approach to customer retention** by analyzing customer demographics, financial profiles, behavior, and credit risk to identify churn patterns and recommend retention strategies.

Target users:
- Business Analysts  
- Banking & Finance Teams  
- Risk & Retention Teams  
- Marketing & CRM Teams  

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

The dashboard was built using:

- 📊 **Power BI Desktop** – Dashboard development and visualization  
- 🔄 **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation  
- 🧠 **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – Measures, KPIs, Columns and churn calculations  
- 🗂️ **Data Modeling** – Table relationships and cross-filtering  
- 📁 **File Format** – `.pbix` and `.png` preview images  

---

## 📂 Dataset Information

Dataset: **Bank Customer Churn Dataset**

The dataset contains customer-level information including:

- Customer demographics (Age, Gender, Geography)
- Account details and balances
- Credit score and financial profile
- Number of banking products
- Credit card ownership
- Active vs inactive status
- Customer churn flag

## 📉 Business Problem

Banks lose significant revenue when customers leave.  
However, identifying **why customers churn** and **who is most at risk** is challenging without proper analytics.

Key questions addressed:
- Which customers are most likely to churn?
- Which demographics have the highest churn risk?
- How does credit score affect retention?
- Do balance and product usage impact churn?
- What actions can reduce churn?

---

## 🎯 Goal of the Dashboard

To build an **interactive churn analytics solution** that:

- Tracks overall churn performance
- Identifies high-risk customer segments
- Understands behavioral churn drivers
- Provides actionable retention strategies

# 📊 Dashboard Walkthrough

---

# 🟢 Page 1 — Customer Retention & Risk Monitoring

### 🔑 Key KPIs
- **10K Total Customers**
- **2K Churned Customers**
- **20.37% Churn Rate**
- **51.51% Active Customers**
- **Average Balance: $76.49K**
- **Average Credit Score: 650**

---

## 👥 Customer Demographics

### Churn by Age Group
- **Age 40–60 shows the highest churn concentration (~35–40%)**
- Older customers also show elevated churn risk.

### Churn by Geography
- Germany shows the **highest churn rate (~32%)**
- Spain & France show lower churn levels.

### Gender Distribution
Balanced churn distribution across male and female customers.

---

## 📊 Customer Behaviour Analysis

### Churn by Number of Products
- Customers with **only 1 product have the highest churn**
- Multi-product customers are significantly more loyal.

### Credit Card Ownership
- Customers **without a credit card churn ~1.5x more**
- Credit card adoption improves retention.

---

## 💰 Financial Profile of Customers

- Churned customers hold **$91K avg balance**
- Retained customers hold **$73K avg balance**

➡️ High-balance churn creates **major revenue risk**.

## 💳 Churn by Balance Segment

Highest churn segment:
- Customers with **1–50K balance (34.6%)**

This segment represents a **major retention opportunity**.

---

## 🔥 Customer Profile Heatmap  
**Age Group vs Credit Score vs Churn**

Key findings:
- Customers aged **40–60 have the highest churn risk**
- Low credit score + middle age = **highest churn segment**

---

# 💡 Key Insights

- Customers with **low credit scores churn ~2x more** than high-score customers.  
- **High-balance customers contribute heavily to revenue loss** when they churn.  
- **Single-product customers show ~50% higher churn** than multi-product users.  
- Customers **without credit cards churn 1.5x more**.  
- **Age group 40–60 has the highest churn concentration**.  
- **Inactive customers churn ~3x more** than active customers.

---

# 🚀 Recommended Business Actions

- 🎯 Target **low credit score & age 40–60 segment** with retention campaigns  
- 💎 Introduce **VIP loyalty programs** for high-balance customers  
- 🔄 Launch **cross-sell campaigns** to increase product adoption  
- 💳 Promote **credit card usage & digital engagement**  
- 📣 Run re-engagement campaigns for inactive customers

This dashboard enables banks to:

- Protect revenue by identifying **high-value customers at risk**
